POOLED OR WITHIN-PAPER?

Most of the spread belongs to the paper, not the compound.

A pooled censored model treats every record as a draw from one distribution. Medicinal-chemistry papers are not draws from one distribution: each reports a congeneric series built around one scaffold and one hypothesis. The grouped model gives every paper its own mean and asks the papers to share only a within-paper spread. The shift is how far each paper's exact-only mean overstates its censored estimate.

CENSORED RECORDS

A bound below the threshold cannot say whether a compound is weak. Its share is a direct measure of how much the comparison depends on top concentrations chosen by each laboratory.

COMPARE TWO STRATA

A pooled difference answers a question about which papers exist.

Pick two targets (or any stratum column). The pooled difference compares everything ever reported for each. The within-paper difference compares them only inside papers that measured both, weighted as in a Mantel–Haenszel estimate. When the two disagree in sign, the pooled number is describing the literature's composition.

DISGUISED CEILINGS

If bounds were written as values, the top of each paper would tie.

When a paper records "inactive at 10 µM" as "IC50 = 10 µM," several compounds share its highest exact value. The check compares how often a paper's top exact value is tied by at least three compounds with how often an interior value is, as an exact expectation over a randomly chosen interior record.

BOUNDARIES

What the check does not do.

Scope. It reads relations and values; it does not read dose-response curves, so it cannot tell whether an exact value was truly bracketed. The censored models assume normal potencies within a group and an independent, known bound for each censored record. They are estimates of direction and scale for a dataset, not corrected potencies for any compound. The primary and counter-screen labels are operational: the stratum with the highest median pIC50 in a paper is called primary. Software verification is not biological validation.
