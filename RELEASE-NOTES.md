# Release notes

## v0.1.0-alpha

- First release, as companion to Data-Rich, Insight-Poor · The Audit, No. 1, "Where the Inactives Went."
- Filter accounting, weak-tail shares (record-weighted, paper-weighted and reachable), bound positions, pooled and grouped censored-normal models, pooled versus within-paper strata comparison, counter-screen censoring, disguised-ceiling test, compound-level collapse.
- Bundled ChEMBL journal subset for six targets (CC BY-SA 3.0).
- 24 tests, including regression tests against the published numbers.
- Robustness script `analysis/depth.mjs`: paper-resampled intervals, threshold and era sensitivity, selection and weighting decomposition, ceiling–potency association, truncated-normal bias prediction.
